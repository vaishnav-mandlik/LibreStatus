Redesigned app, now the same as the Google Play version, renamed Status Saver.

* New look with light and dark themes
* Save many statuses at once, and repost or share any of them
* Auto-save new statuses when you open the app
* Favourites, and a Vault that keeps saved statuses out of the gallery
* Built-in photo viewer and video player
* No permissions at all on Android 11 and later
