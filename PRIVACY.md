# Privacy policy for Status Downloader・Video Saver

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Status Downloader・Video Saver, which appears on your phone as "Status Saver",
is an Android app that shows the status photos and videos your messaging app
has already downloaded to your phone, and lets you keep the ones you want.
Everything it does happens on your device. In this policy it is called "the
app".

## Not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta

Status Downloader・Video Saver is an independent app. It is not affiliated
with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or in any way officially connected to WhatsApp
LLC, Meta Platforms, Inc., or any of their subsidiaries or affiliates.
"WhatsApp" and "Meta" and all related names, marks and logos are the property
of their respective owners, and are referred to here only to describe what
this app is compatible with.

The app does not modify, patch, automate or interfere with any messaging app.
It does not read your messages or your contacts, and it has no internet
permission, so it cannot communicate with anyone's servers. The only data
belonging to another app that it reads is the status media your messaging app
has already downloaded and stored in its folder on your own device, and only
after you grant access yourself through Android's own folder picker.

Statuses belong to the people who posted them. Please respect their privacy and
the copyright of anything you save or reshare.

## What we collect

Nothing.

The app has no internet permission, so it is not able to send any data
anywhere. There is no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting,
no accounts and no sign-in. The developer receives nothing from the app.

## What the app reads and keeps on your device

- **Your messaging app's status folder.** On Android 11 and later you pick the
  Android › media folder in Android's own folder picker. Android then lets the
  app read everything inside that folder, but the app only opens the messaging
  app's status folders in it. On Android 10 and earlier the app uses Android's
  storage permission instead, for the same purpose.
- **The copies you save.** Statuses you choose to keep are written to the
  `Status` album inside `DCIM` in your gallery. The app only deletes copies it
  wrote itself.
- **The Vault.** Statuses you move to the Vault are copied into the app's
  private storage and removed from your gallery where Android allows it. If
  Android does not allow it, the app tells you the gallery copy is still there.
  Vault files are hidden from other apps, not encrypted.
- **Favourites and settings**, such as theme, language and auto-save. These are
  stored on the device only.

On Android 11 and later the app asks for no storage or media permission at
all. On Android 10 and earlier it asks for Android's storage permission, which
Android describes as access to photos, media and files; the app uses it only to
read the status folder and your saved `Status` album. The app never asks for
"All files access".

## What the app does not do

- It does not upload, sync or back up anything.
- It does not read your messages or your contacts.
- It does not track you, and it shows no advertising.
- It does not use any third-party service.

## Sharing and links

Nothing leaves the app unless you tap something to send it, and then it goes
only where you send it:

- **Share** opens Android's share sheet and hands the file to the app you pick.
- **Repost** hands a single status straight to your messaging app. Several
  statuses at once go through the share sheet.
- **Open chat** passes the phone number you typed to your messaging app through
  its own link. If that app does not answer the link, the number opens as a
  `wa.me` web link in whichever app handles it, usually your browser.
- **Share app** opens Android's share sheet with the app's name and its Google
  Play link, for you to send to someone.
- **Send feedback** opens your email app with the app version and Android
  version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

What happens after that is governed by the receiving app's or service's own
privacy policy, not this one.

## Data retention and deletion

Favourites, settings and everything in the Vault are stored only in the app's
private storage on your phone. They stay until you remove them in the app,
clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, which
deletes all of it, including the Vault. The app keeps no backup and has
nowhere to keep one.

Copies you saved to the `Status` album in your gallery are ordinary files. They
stay after you uninstall the app, until you delete them.

To withdraw the folder access you granted, clear the app's storage in Android
Settings › Apps › Status Saver › Storage, or uninstall the app.

If you email feedback, the developer keeps that email only as long as needed to
answer it. To have it deleted, write to vaishnavmandlik51@duck.com.

## Security

Nothing leaves your phone through the app, because it has no internet
permission. Vault files are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps
cannot read. They are hidden, not encrypted, so anyone who can unlock your
phone and open the app can see them.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use the media you keep. Please respect the
privacy of the people who posted it, and the copyright of anything you reshare.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with
a new date at the top.
